# Research Survey

## Responsible AI-Driven Personalisation in B2B Marketing: The Role of Transparency and Trust in Customer Engagement

### About the Study

This academic study examines how AI-driven personalisation is experienced in business-to-business (B2B) marketing and how transparency and trust relate to customer engagement.

For this study, **AI-driven personalisation** refers to the use of AI and data-driven technologies to personalise marketing communication, content, recommendations, offers or digital interactions according to the needs and interests of business customers.

Examples may include personalised emails, product or service recommendations, customised website content, AI-enabled chatbots, account-specific content, personalised offers and automated follow-up communication.

There are no right or wrong answers. Please respond based on your professional experience. Your responses will be used only for academic research and analysed in aggregate.
